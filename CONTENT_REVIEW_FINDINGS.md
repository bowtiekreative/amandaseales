# Content Review: Kamala Harris Misinformation / Vote Discouragement & Aspie Supremacy

**Scope:** All 2,434 `.srt` transcripts in `Amanda_Seales_Transcripts/` and `Video by amandaseales(1070)/`.
**Method:** Full-corpus keyword sweeps (Kamala/Harris — 90 files; autism/aspie/neurotypical/neurodivergent — 59 files; vote-discouragement phrases — 55 files; superiority language — 84 files), followed by manual review of extracted context windows around every hit.

**Important caveat about the corpus:** it mixes (a) Amanda Seales's own videos/lives, and (b) third-party commentary videos *about* her (critics, reaction channels, interviews). Each finding below is labeled accordingly. "Misinformation" is reserved for checkable false or unsupported factual claims; harsh opinion and characterization are noted as such but are not the same thing.

## Part 1 — Aspie Supremacy (direct and implied)

Background: "Aspie supremacy" is the ableist belief that "high-functioning"/low-support-needs autistic people are *superior* — to neurotypical people and/or to other autistic people. The term was coined critically by Mel Baggs in 2010. It is almost never stated outright; it shows up as implied framing: autism as the source of genius/talent, neurotypicals as lesser/controlled minds, autistic people as harder to fool or "more evolved."

## Summary judgment

- **Aspie supremacy:** No one in the corpus says "aspies are superior" outright — as expected, the language is implied. The strongest pattern is in Seales's own lives: autism framed as proof of multi-talent/genius (1.2), neurotypicals framed as drones/clones/controlled minds (1.3), neurodivergence framed as immunity to indoctrination (1.4), plus the on-record moment where a supporter called it "an autism supremacist point of view" and was blocked (1.1).
- **Kamala Harris:** The bulk of the content is harsh opinion (legitimate, if inflammatory). The items that cross into misinformation are: repeated unsupported election-rigging claims; the factually false "she married into" career claim; "debunked lies"/"false allegations" claims asserted as settled fact; and the literal-false "didn't say two words about the working class." Vote discouragement is mostly indirect (delegitimizing voting, "slave mentality," "you were an op") with a few direct instances ("not voting on an electoral level," "they can't win if you don't vote"), concentrated in 2025–2026 content — while her 2022–2024 content urged people to vote. The most extreme Harris-related misinformation in the corpus (vaccine-depopulation conspiracy) comes from a hostile third-party channel, not from Seales.
